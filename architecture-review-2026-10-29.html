Problem. A shallow member-list interface leaves enrichment and role freshness with callers.

Solution. Put membership assembly and refresh behind one module seam; keep presence separate.

  • Tests cover role refresh

Decision to settle: when role and profile data refresh, especially after ownership transfer.

Top recommendation

One server membership view

Four active callers repeat assembly, and stale roles affect action visibility. A deeper membership module gives immediate locality and an interface that can test refresh after role changes.
